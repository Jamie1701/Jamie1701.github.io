About

I work in scientific machine learning, with a particular interest in decoding black-box systems through explainable AI. I'm currently with AWE Nuclear Security Technologies, applying this to nuclear test-ban verification — a novel explainable ensemble for seismic event discrimination, built in support of Comprehensive Test Ban Treaty enforcement, paired with a cross-architecture explainability suite that traces its predictions back to physically meaningful source-physics regions. Earlier work spans generative design of MAX phase and MXene materials — materials of interest for warhead construction — energy systems engineering, computational chemistry, and statistical/solid-state physics. This page collects the papers, projects, and technical reports from that work.

I'm fluent in PyTorch and Scikit-learn, with a strong foundation across the scientific Python stack — Pandas and Polars for data processing, NumPy and SciPy for numerical computing — and have experience distributing model training across multi-GPU and multi-CPU nodes using Bash-scripted job arrays on HPC clusters.

Geothermal Energy and Underground Thermal Energy Storage

P02

icferst · reservoir-simulation · geothermal · 2026

Evaluated the performance of an Aquifer Thermal Energy Storage (ATES) system in a fractured granite reservoir using the IC-FERST simulator over a five-year operating period, assessing thermal recovery, heat-pump electricity demand, and CO₂ savings relative to gas heating across two demand scenarios. Optimisation of an Iron Redox Flow Cell Cathode via Electrolyte Cation and pH Screening

P13

electrochemistry · energy-storage · materials-science · 2025

Investigated cathode performance in a 1mM hydrated iron(II) sulphate redox flow cell using cyclic voltammetry, screening four electrolyte cations (Cs₂, Li₂, K₂, Na₂ sulphates) before optimising pH within the acidic region for the best-performing electrolyte. Potassium sulphate at pH ≈ 3.5 gave peak oxidation and reduction currents roughly 40% higher than the unoptimised system, with the improvement discussed in terms of Marcus theory and proton-coupled electron transfer.

A NumPy Multilayer Perceptron for Crystal Structure Space Group Prediction

P14

python · numpy · machine-learning · 2024

Built a multilayer perceptron from scratch using only NumPy — no ML framework dependency — to predict crystal space group from Magpie-derived compositional features, reaching roughly 70% accuracy across ~3,300 crystal structures spanning 144 possible space groups.

Deep Learning for Computational Analysis of Crystal Structures: A Review

P15

literature-review · deep-learning · crystallography · 2025

Reviewed three deep-learning approaches to crystal structure analysis of increasing scope: a neural-network potential-energy-surface model benchmarked against MP2 for ice polymorphs, a classification-and-regression ensemble (CRYSPNet) predicting Bravais lattice, space group and lattice parameters for inorganic crystals — including a correct blind prediction for a previously misassigned rare-earth perovskite — and a neural network emulating DFT to predict atomic and electronic properties of organic molecules, polymer chains and crystals at linear rather than cubic computational cost. Identifies a common limitation across all three studies: none disclose model training-time overhead, which the review argues undermines their claimed efficiency gains.

Computational Study of Cyclohexane Conformer Stability and Substituent Effects

P16

computational-chemistry · quantum-chemistry · dft · 2024

Used Hartree-Fock (3-21G basis set) static calculations and coordinate scans to examine axial/equatorial conformer preference and half-chair transition energetics for cyclohexane and four substituted derivatives (methyl, ethynyl, trifluoromethyl, carbonitrile), benchmarking against published experimental and MP2 results. Found the method a reasonable, computationally cheap approximation for electronically simple systems, but identified systematic misprediction for substituents with strong electron correlation or diffuse-orbital effects, attributed to known HF/3-21G limitations.
